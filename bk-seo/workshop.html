Four fixes. Paste-ready.

Everything from the workshop, in the order to do it. Give each one about an hour this week.

1. One line, everywhere

Fill in the four boxes and your line appears here.

Paste the same wording into: Google Business Profile, HAR.com, Zillow, Realtor.com, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, your own page and your email signature.

Changed brokerage, team or phone since you made a profile? Edit the old ones. Don't delete them.

2. A bio AI can quote

Paste this into your AI chatbot, with your current bio where the brackets are.

Here is my bio: [paste your bio] Rewrite only the first three sentences so they include my full name, my brokerage, my city, who I help and one proof point. Use only facts already in the bio. Keep my voice. Leave the rest alone.

Check the result: who you are, where you work, who you help and one proof, all inside the first three sentences. Then read it out loud. If you wouldn't say it on the phone, change it.

3. Three answers on your own page

Find the questions your clients ask before they call anyone, then answer three of them.

I'm a real estate agent in [city] who works with [client type]. List ten questions these clients ask before they call an agent. Then draft a 60-word answer to the three I pick. Start each answer with the direct answer. Leave a blank for any number so I can verify it. End each one with my name, brokerage and city.

  • The question is the heading, in the client's words.
  • The answer comes first.
  • One local fact with a number. Check it at the source before you publish.
  • Your name, brokerage and city, inside the answer.

Put them on a page you control: your website or a landing page in your CRM. Typed text, not a picture of text and not a PDF.

Then test the page

Ask ChatGPT to read my page →

If it can't tell you who wrote the page, fix that first. Passing is a good sign, not proof: other AI readers can still miss content that loads late. Plain typed text on the page is what works everywhere.

Optional: label the answers for machines

Turn these three questions and answers into FAQ schema in JSON-LD format, ready to paste into my page's custom code box: [paste your three questions and answers]

Five minutes, and optional. Google stopped showing FAQ results in Search in May 2026. The code still labels your content for machines.

4. Reviews, asked the right way

Hi [first name], it's [your name]. Handing you those keys made my month. If you have two minutes, would you share your experience in a Google review? Say whatever you'd like, in your own words. [your review link]

  • Ask every client, the same way. Text it the day they get the keys.
  • Don't tell them what to write. No names, no keywords, no star count. Google's review policy has barred that since April 2026.
  • Nothing in return for a review. Ever.
  • A steady few each month beats twenty in a week.
  • Reply to each one in a sentence.
