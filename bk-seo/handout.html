How Google and AI decide which agent gets recommended · Part 1 of 2

Engine 1 · SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

  1. Crawl. Google's bot reads your website, profiles and pages that mention you.
  2. Index. It files who you are, where you work, what you do.
  3. Rank. It pulls the best match for each search.

Google's three local factors: relevance (does your profile match the search), distance (how close you are), prominence (reviews, ratings, links, mentions). Google says there's no way to pay for a better local ranking.

Quality = E-E-A-T: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness. Trust matters most, and home buying is a money decision held to a higher bar.

Engine 2 · AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

  1. Reads the ask: city, price range, specialty.
  2. Fans out: runs several searches at once (Google calls it “query fan-out”).
  3. Reads sources: websites, Google profiles, Yelp, Zillow, reviews, news.
  4. Cross-checks: who shows up in many places, consistently, with strong reviews.
  5. Answers: names a few and shows where it looked.

AI doesn't rank, it selects. On Google, reviews move you up. In AI answers, reviews decide whether you're included at all.

Four things you control

1 · FindableGoogle and AI can reach and read you: a claimed Google Business Profile and a real bio on your own website, in text (not baked into images).
2 · ConsistentSame name, brokerage, phone, service areas and specialties on every profile. One study found only 68% of business contact details in ChatGPT and Perplexity matched the Google profile³.
3 · TrustedSteady, recent reviews on Google and the real estate sites, and a reply to every one.
4 · QuotableClear answers, on your site, to the questions clients really ask about your area. If you answer it, AI has something to cite.
