Does AI name you?

Ask the question a buyer would ask, and see who comes back. About a minute.

1. Who does AI recommend in your city?

Type your city and the question appears here.

Ask ChatGPT → Ask Google AI Mode →

Look for your name in the answer. In class, go back to the chat and type 1 if it named you, 0 if it didn't.

No names at all? Ask who has the best reviews instead →

2. What does AI know about you?

Type your name (and your city above) and the question appears here.

Ask ChatGPT → Ask Google AI Mode →

Screenshot the answer. Note anything wrong or out of date: old brokerage, old phone number, wrong city. Those are the fixes we start with in the workshop.

Before the workshop

  1. Run both checks above and screenshot every answer.
  2. Fill in the scorecard on page 2 of the class handout.
  3. Join the workshop from a laptop with your logins open: Google Business Profile, your website, Zillow, Realtor.com, HAR.com.

What you type stays on your device. This page only builds the question and opens it for you.
