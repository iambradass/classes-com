HOW I INTRODUCE MYSELF: [YOUR NAME, BROKERAGE, AND WHAT YOU DO]

Give me, short and in plain English:

Prompt 4

Build your lake field sheet

The questions to ask on a lake property, and who actually answers each one.

Stay on this same property. Build me a one-screen field sheet of what to check on a lake home like this. It is on Cedar Creek Lake, a Tarrant Regional Water District (TRWD) reservoir.

Group the questions under:
- Water and shoreline (dock and bulkhead permits, flood easement, water levels)
- Systems (septic or sewer, water source, storm and flood history)
- Community (HOA rules, rentals, golf and marina access and fees)
- Insurance and taxes
- Title and survey (easements, restrictions, encroachments)

For each question, say who answers it: listing agent, HOA, TRWD, inspector, insurance agent, surveyor, title company, or attorney.

Then star the 5 that matter most. Keep it tight enough to read on a phone. Do not state legal conclusions or quote rules you aren't sure of. Tell me where to confirm instead.

Been to my class? Go further: Add: "Turn the top 5 into a client-ready risk register: the risk, what we do about it, and the decision deadline."
