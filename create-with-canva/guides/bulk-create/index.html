A Quarter of Posts in One Hour: Bulk Create

Pick one kind of post you can repeat every week. Get the list from any AI tool. Let Canva build all the posts at once. Then get on with your day.

About 1 hour for 12 weeks of posts7 steps

  4. Bulk CreateOne list in, all the posts out.
  5. Skim and stopFive minutes, not fifty.
  6. You walk away withA quarter of posts from one list and one design.

You walk away withTwelve weeks of posts, all in one design, ready to schedule.

What you need

  • A computer. Bulk Create does not run on the phone app.
  • A paid Canva plan or a brokerage account.
  • The file from class, or your own list. The class file is on the hub under Files.
  • A timer. One hour, then you are done.

The steps

One step open at a time. Click "Done, next" to move on. The grey boxes show what the screen looks like; they are pictures, not buttons.

0 of 7 done

  1. Social media rewards showing up every week, not showing up perfectly. So pick one kind of post: a funny real estate quote, a homeownership tip, a "did you know" about buying. One idea, every week, same day. That is what people remember.

  2. Open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and paste prompt 5. Ask for 12 for a quarter, or 52 for a year. Download the file it gives you.

    Open the file and look at the very top. The column names must be in the first row, with nothing above them. A title line above the names is what tripped up the demo in class. Read the list, and delete any line you would not say out loud.

    How the file should lookSAMPLE
    QuoteTheme
    1A "motivated seller" is just a homeowner who has refreshed Zillow one too many times.Sellers
    2Every buyer wants "move-in ready" until they see what move-in ready costs.Buyers

  3. In Canva AI, paste prompt 6. Pick ONE design and stop looking. Keep one text box where the quote goes, plus your name or logo. Delete everything else. Brand tab, Shuffle, until it is in your colors.

    The same look every week is how people know it is you before they read a word.

  7. Click Continue and let Canva build. Ten lines become ten pages, each one a finished post. Fifty-two become fifty-two.

    Skim every page. A long quote may run off the edge; make the text a little smaller on that page and move on. Give this five minutes, not fifty. Then schedule one page a week with the next guide.

Before you goPage 3 of 3

Watch out for

  1. The title row breaks it.

    Column names go in row one with nothing above them. That was the whole problem in class.

  2. Excel works too.

    Brad said in class to use CSV. Either kind of file works. CSV just cannot carry pictures.

  3. Up to 300 lines at a time.

    Plenty. That is almost six years of weekly posts.

  4. Set the timer.

    The point of Bulk Create is to give you your hour back. Do not spend it making ten designs.
